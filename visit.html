Visit the Monastery

Visitors are welcome to Ging Gompa throughout the year. The monastery is approached by road from Darjeeling via Lebong. Visitors are requested to maintain silence within the prayer halls, dress modestly, and seek permission before photographing monks or rituals.

Early mornings and late afternoons tend to offer the most contemplative experience, when the rhythms of prayer and the surrounding landscape are most apparent.

Visitor Etiquette

  • Remove shoes before entering prayer halls.
  • Maintain silence and avoid loud conversation within monastery premises.
  • Photography inside prayer halls or during rituals should only be done with permission.
  • Respect ongoing rituals and avoid interrupting monastic activities.
  • Dress modestly and conduct oneself with awareness of the sacred setting.
