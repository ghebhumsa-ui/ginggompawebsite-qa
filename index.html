Visit

Ging, Darjeeling

The monastery is located approximately 10 km from Darjeeling town along the Lebong route, in a quiet setting approached through forest, pine, and tea-estate roads.

Location: Ging, Darjeeling, West Bengal

Route: Darjeeling town to Lebong, then toward Ging Lama Hatta

Before visiting: Contact the monastery for group visits or documentation work
