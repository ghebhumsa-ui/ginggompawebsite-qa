Support & Preservation

Contributions to Sangchen Thongdrol Ling (Ging Gompa) support the preservation of a historically significant monastery and the continuity of its monastic and educational traditions.

Areas of Support

  • Structural restoration and maintenance of the monastery
  • Education and daily sustenance of resident monks (shedra)
  • Protection of remaining monastery land
  • Preservation of archival records and heritage material
